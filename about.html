About

Research-driven. Impact-focused.

An educator and AI/ML researcher working at the intersection of intelligent systems and real-world data.

I am an Assistant Professor and AI/ML researcher with over 17 years of experience in teaching, research, mentoring, and academic coordination.

My academic and research interests lie at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Streaming Data Analytics, with a particular focus on adaptive learning, concept drift, model adaptation, and intelligent data analysis.

My research work focuses on developing reliable and adaptive approaches for non-stationary data streams and exploring practical AI/ML solutions to real-world problems.

Alongside research, I am passionate about teaching and mentoring students, guiding projects, and creating meaningful learning experiences.

I have contributed to academic and professional activities through research publications, conference presentations, student mentoring, academic coordination, and innovation initiatives. I am particularly interested in research collaboration, academic opportunities, and projects that connect intelligent technologies with practical applications.
