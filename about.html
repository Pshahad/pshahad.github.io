About

Research-driven. Impact-focused.

An educator and AI/ML researcher working on how learning systems detect and adapt to change in real-world data.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of AI & ML at JAIN (Deemed-to-be University), Bengaluru, with over 17 years of experience in teaching, research, mentoring and academic leadership. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT Kottayam in October 2026.

My research focuses on learning from non-stationary data streams: detecting concept drift and change points reliably and early, and adapting models without losing transparency. Current interests include sequential change-point detection, interpretable and responsible machine learning, uncertainty quantification under distribution shift, and resource-efficient inference.

My work has appeared in journals such as Knowledge-Based Systems (Elsevier) and Computing (Springer), and has received two IEEE Best Paper Awards (ICSCAN 2021 and ICEC 2024). I have also co-authored the book Data Streaming and Analysis: From Real-Time Data to Powerful Insights (2026).

Teaching is a long-standing strength. I have taught Machine Learning, Data Science, Business Analytics and Data Mining, Python programming and core computing courses, and I mentor student project teams, including a first-runner-up team at the CSI-InApp International Student Project Awards. I also serve as a resource person for programmes on research methodology, academic writing and machine learning, and as a reviewer for international conferences.

I welcome research collaboration and academic opportunities that connect rigorous methods with practical, data-intensive applications.
